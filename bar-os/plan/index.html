Answered in advance

Everything you might ask, already answered.

The questions that come up on the way to a first Monday — here up front, so nobody has to chase an answer.

QWhat do I actually post — and when?

The calendar tells you exactly. Every piece is tagged with the day it fires and why. If you only do three things a week: a Thursday intro, a Monday-morning "tonight" reminder, and a Tuesday recap.

See the calendar →

QWhich poster is which?

Six formats, each with one job — the IG single for the weekly punch, the story for day-of, the carousel for the swipe, the reel for reach, the print poster for the door, the website hero for the full read. All labeled.

See the posters →

QDo people sign up ahead of time?

No. Come as you will — walk in, put your name down by 7, ten bucks, get drawn a partner. No app, no reservation, no list to manage. That's the whole point of a blind draw.

QWhat does it cost, and what's the pot?

$10 a player. Clover adds $50. Winner takes the pot; second gets their buy-in back. The $1 4-Bagger Pot runs separately all night — carryover jackpot, the night's big moment.

How the money works →

QWhat if nobody shows the first time?

It's planned for. Run it anyway — small and loud. A handful of teams still fills the boards, and the recap seeds the next week. The playbook has the thin-night version (and the packed-night one).

If it goes sideways →

QSomething needs to change — a time, a price?

Just say the word. One update flows to every post, poster, and email, and it's back out the same week — under 48 hours. You never chase a fix across a pile of files.
