# Monday Bags — IG Reel Concept (the discovery asset)

> **Format job:** Reels are Instagram's discovery engine — **2–3× the reach of static, ~55% of
> views from non-followers** (research `D-format-and-attention.md` §2). This is how cold people
> who've never heard of Clover find Monday Bags. The **first frame carries ~80% of the weight**;
> its only job is to **stop the scroll and earn the next second** — not to explain the night.
> Top 2026 ranking signals: **watch-time · sends-per-reach (DM shares) · likes-per-reach** — so
> we build for completion (short, every beat lands sound-off) and bake in a **share prompt**.

**Can't fully produce yet** — there's no night footage (night 1 drew nobody; first real night is
**Mon Jul 6**). This doc is the **shoot script a Clover staffer can run on a phone** at that night.
The paired `ig-reel-firstframe.html` is the designed **cover frame** (and a usable static teaser
until footage exists). Voice: terse, swagger, short, **zero emoji**.

---

## The chosen hook (first frame)

**"POV: you walked in alone."**

Over the packed bar mid-erupt (the room you walked *into*). The tension is the whole hook: you
came with nobody — and look at this. POV framing is a proven Reel scroll-stopper, and the
contrast (alone ↔ a room losing its mind) creates the curiosity gap that earns second 2.
Rendered in `ig-reel-firstframe.html`.

## The 3-beat arc

1. **WALK IN ALONE → THE DRAW** — you came solo; we pull you a random partner (you've never met).
2. **THE THROW → THE ROOM** — first night, a bag drops, the bar goes off. *Anybody can win.*
3. **THE POT → CTA + SHARE** — winners take the pot; "every Monday, just walk in — send this to
   your Monday crew."

Total runtime **~24s** (Reels sweet spot 15–30s; sub-30 protects completion rate). Everything
filmable on a phone, vertical 9:16, at one real Monday night.

## Trending-audio strategy

- **Use a trending audio track** — the audio itself is a distribution signal. In the Reels editor,
  pick from the audio library with the **↗ trending arrow**; favor an upbeat, high-energy track
  that loops cleanly under ~30s.
- **Land a beat-drop on a moment** — sync the track's hit to either the **bag dropping in (0:08–0:11)**
  or the **room erupting (0:14)**. That sync is what makes a phone-shot reel feel produced.
- **Keep real ambience low underneath** — the *crack* of the bag and the live cheer add authenticity;
  layer them under the track at low volume rather than replacing it. A trending track travels
  further than original audio for a cold-reach push.
- **Design for muted first views** — never rely on the audio to carry meaning; the text overlays do
  the work. Audio is reach + vibe, not information.

## Posting cadence (ties to `release-timeline.md`)

- **Production gate:** the motion reel needs real footage → **first shoot = Mon Jul 6 (Week 1, first
  real night).** Until then, `ig-reel-firstframe.html` runs as a **static story teaser** in Week 0
  ("Mondays. you'll see.") — the reel itself *waits for footage*. Do not fake it.
- **First post:** **Thu Jul 9** (the Week 1 **anchor / "what is this"** slot) — Thursday catches
  weekend planners and is the week's reach driver. The reel *is* the anchor that week, replacing the
  static intro. It's evergreen discovery, not a dated recap, so it keeps working after.
- **Reinforce:** re-share to **story Mon ~10–11am** as part of the day-of reminder (the night-1 fix —
  early, never 2:30pm).
- **Refresh monthly** (timeline "refresh visuals monthly") — recut with fresh night footage so it
  never reads stale. One strong evergreen reel + periodic new cuts.
- **Format mix (research D §2):** ~**60% Reels / 25% carousels / 15% static**, 4–6 posts/week. The
  reel carries the reach load; the carousel earns saves; static punctuates the weekly conversion.

## Alternate hooks (swap the first frame, same arc)

1. **"Nobody here came with a partner."** — over the crowd/erupt shot. More literal; same tension,
   slightly less swagger than the POV cut.
2. **"First time touching a bag. Watch what happens."** — over the throw/make. Leads with the
   de-risk angle ("I'm not good") instead of the solo angle ("no one to go with").
3. **"We pair you with a stranger. You might take the whole pot."** — over the two-strangers dap.
   Leads with stakes. *(Keep the pot a phrase, never a baked dollar figure — the pot is variable.)*

## Why this beats a static poster (the one-line rationale)

A static poster under-uses Instagram — it converts the people who already follow Clover. The reel
is the **discovery engine** that reaches the 55% who don't, sells the **blast** (not the rules),
and turns its own viewers into distributors via the share prompt. It's the top of the whole funnel
the rest of the suite converts.
