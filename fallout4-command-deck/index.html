The Faction Sequence — "Minutemen-First"

You're the player; the agent's the friend with the whole game memorized. Read to get smart, then drive.

The plain question

Whose endgame ending are you taking? The game won't let you keep all four factions alive — whichever path you pick kills the others. We've been calling it "Minutemen-first." What does that actually mean?

What it IS (from zero)

Fallout 4 has four factions: Minutemen (the people's militia you've been building), Brotherhood of Steel (Maxson's flying-fortress crew), Railroad (synth liberation — you wiped them), and Institute (underground tech overlords, where your "son" Father runs the show).

Each faction has its own version of an endgame quest called The Nuclear Option. Whichever version you do, that's "your" ending. The others die or become irrelevant.

The Nuclear Option (Minutemen version), in plain terms: Sturges figures out how to sneak into the Institute through an old cooling-water pipe. You go in with Preston + a Minutemen squad, fight through synth soldiers, gorillas (they have a bio lab), and elite Courser synths, reach the reactor at the bottom, plant a fusion pulse charge, teleport out via their relay, and press the detonator. The whole underground city goes up like a small nuke. The Minutemen become the unchallenged power in the Commonwealth.

It's literally the "blow up the bad guys' base" ending. ~30–45 min of gameplay, big cinematic. The Institute becomes a crater on your world map for the rest of the game.

Why this matters for Carl

  • DCC parallel: Institute = Borant Corporation — the system that treats people as test subjects. Carl burns it down. Ideological keystone of the run.
  • The Minutemen win: Preston, Sturges, Ronnie Shaw, the settlers get to be the heroes. The people's army — not Maxson, not aristocrats.
  • Father dies on screen. You confront him in his quarters as he's dying of cancer (CHA 7 opens dialogue easily). The closest the game gets to a quiet moment with the "son" it's been jerking you around about for 50 hours.

What the world looks like after

  • ☠️ Institute reduced to rubble — visible crater, all named Institute NPCs dead
  • 🛡️ Brotherhood stays non-hostile — destroying the Institute fails their active quests but does NOT auto-aggro them; you provoke them yourself by boarding the Prydwen and opening fire (Warlord Phase 3)
  • 🤝 Minutemen own the Commonwealth — Preston's speech, Radio Freedom broadcasts your victory
  • ❤️ Sanctuary settlers and Castle defenders cheer you on

Why this order (not Brotherhood-first)

  1. Doctrine: the Institute is the ideological enemy. Killing them via the people's army is the thematically right answer. Brotherhood-first means handing the Commonwealth to the fascist airship captain, then wiping him too — the messy way.
  2. Locks: the Brotherhood path requires advancing "Spoils of War," which boards a BoS vertibird to Mass Fusion and auto-locks the Minutemen ending. There's no Brotherhood-first path that keeps Minutemen options. So if Minutemen is the goal, they go first.
Point of no return

Once you trigger Banished from the Institute: Institute turns permanently hostile (no more apartment/vendors), the "Airship Down" quest becomes unavailable (fine — you don't want it), and X6-88 turns hostile. Once you press the Nuclear Option detonator, the Institute is gone forever.

Agent's honest pick

Minutemen-first is the right call — fits Carl's doctrine, gives the cleanest Brotherhood follow-up, and the Minutemen Nuclear Option is genuinely one of the best quests in the game.

The plan has moved to the Raider Warlord Apocalypse. The Nuclear Option (Minutemen) described here is still used — it's Warlord Phase 2, the way Carl kills the Institute before turning on the Minutemen themselves. The Minutemen-sovereign ending is archived as the 🛡️ Revert Point.

Below decks: barracks full of power-armored troops and the named officers (Kells the XO, Cade the doctor, Ingram the chief engineer, Quinlan the librarian, Teagan the armorer).

🅰️ Path A — Manual Prydwen Assault (your fantasy)

Board the Prydwen — throw a vertibird signal grenade for a pickup, OR take the Boston Airport elevator up. On the deck: total firefight. Walk bow → command deck, killing every named officer. 🅱️ Path B — With Our Powers Combined (watch from the Castle)

Skip boarding. Build artillery at 5 settlements, order the strike from the Castle radio operator, and watch the Prydwen fall to coordinated cannon fire. Doctrine-pure — but Maxson dies in the wreckage, not by your hand, the named officers don't spawn for the follow-up Castle battle, and you forfeit Final Judgment + the Battlecoat + the personal moment.

🅰️🅱️ Path AB — Have Both (recommended, and what you picked)

  1. Manual assault (Path A): board, kill everyone, loot Maxson, drop the Fat Man on exit.

The L56 Perk — What Each Pick Actually Does

Combat ceiling is reached; this is a utility pick. Set your choice in the Answers panel.

🛢️ Nuclear Physicist (INT 9 — unlocked)

  • Rank 1: Radiation damage you deal +50%; fusion cores last 25% longer.
  • Rank 2: +100% radiation; cores last 50% longer.
  • Rank 3: +100% radiation; cores last 100% longer, AND you can eject fusion cores from your PA as weapons — they detonate like mini-nukes.

Why it's Carl-coded: Kiloton has a built-in radiation component; NP scales it. Fusion-core eject = literally throwing nuke grenades out of your power armor. Doubles PA core duration, which matters since PA is the primary platform.

Caveat — verify before relying on it

The radiation buff should stack with Kiloton's radiation portion, but that interaction needs a Nukapedia check before it's promised as a damage multiplier. The fusion-core eject and PA duration are verified.

🔓 Locksmith (PER 4 — easy)

Ranks open Advanced → Expert → Master locks (+30% sweet spot at 3). Opens loot you currently can't reach (hidden stashes, unique weapons behind locks). Cost-benefit: you're L55 in a post-loot world — useful for completionism, not transformative.

💻 Hacker (INT 4 — already met)

Ranks open Advanced → Expert → Master terminals (never locked out at 3; auto-hack at 4). Unlocks terminal entries, turns turrets against enemies, opens locked doors via terminal. Pure engineer flavor.

Agent's pick — Nuclear Physicist 1

Most Carl-coded on the board, directly useful for PA-heavy endgame (longer core duration through the reactor room + Prydwen), and the fusion-core eject is a new weapon capability — a bigger change than "open more locks." Grab NP2/NP3 later if you like the lane. Honorable mention: Locksmith 1 for immediate world-loot QoL. Skip Hacker for now.

🔧 Mechanic Sidebars

The "wait, how do I actually do that?" answers. The agent gave previews; deeper detail gets dropped in as you ask.

Vertibird signal grenade

A throwable that summons a Brotherhood vertibird (1 grenade per ride). You get them from BoS quartermaster Teagan on the Prydwen (sold to you while friendly). Throw it open-sky outdoors → vertibird arrives in 30–60s → walk to the pilot side door to mount. The side-mounted minigun is usable while flying. The pilot delivers you to a fast-travel destination, or you can jump out mid-flight.

Boston Airport elevator

Alternate Prydwen boarding. Walk to Boston Airport on foot, find the BoS-controlled area, take the elevator up to the Prydwen deck. No grenade needed — slower, but zero resources spent.

Vertibird mounted minigun

The side-door minigun you fire while seated. Solid for clearing ground targets en route. A vertibird you summon = your minigun, your fight.

Captured Minutemen vertibirds

After With Our Powers Combined closes (~2 in-game days), signal grenades summon Minutemen-flown vertibirds with their own gunners — and you can fire from the side door. Flavor on top of the win.
