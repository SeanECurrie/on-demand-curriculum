The model

Free software, paid on the transaction

Fresha gives the booking software away and earns on payments and a marketplace commission. The blended take rate is thin (under one percent on GMV), so the model only works at enormous volume, which is exactly the scale on the prior slide.

The pricing reveal: a $24 sticker becomes roughly $487 a month for a three-person operation once Forms, Branded App, Payroll, and Multi-location stack on.

The floor, honestly

Take the OTE, then divide it by two

OTE ÷ 2

Sales-side reports show roughly 45% of reps hitting quota, but front-line new-business attainment runs materially lower than the org average suggests. Plan finances around base plus 50% of variable, not 100% of on-target earnings.

06
First 90 days

What to ask before you sign

  • What is real new-business quota attainment on this team, not the blended org number?
  • How is the territory defined, and is the marketplace inbound or pure outbound?
  • What does ramp look like, and what is the floor if the marketplace does not convert?
