The deal

Show up alone.

Leave with a crew.

You don't bring a partner — we draw you one at random. That's the whole magic. Two ringers can't team up and run the table. The person who's never thrown a bag might draw the best player in the room. The hotshots have to win even with a rookie on their team. Everybody's got a real shot — and everybody gets better.

It's how our Wednesday darts night became a 15-year staple. Now it's Monday's turn.

How a Monday runs

Four steps. Walk in, get drawn, play, win.
1

Sign up before we start

Walk in, put your name down, grab a drink. No partner needed. Never played? Even better — we'll get you going.

2

Get drawn a partner

Everybody's paired up at random, right there. Luck of the draw — the champ or the rookie. That's the fun.

3

Play the bracket

Double-elimination, games to 21. Everybody plays at least twice — nobody's one-and-done. Two boards going all night.

4

Win the pot

Winning team takes the pot. And all night there's the 4-Bagger Pot — a $1 raffle with a jackpot that grows every week it isn't won.
