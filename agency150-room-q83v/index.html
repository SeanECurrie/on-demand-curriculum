What this is, and how it works

It started with a couple of casual sentences from Jimmy about a company called Agency150 and a meeting where he might invest. The goal isn’t to tell Jimmy what to do — it’s to empower his own decision with verified facts, clearly separating what the company claims from what’s been independently confirmed. The work runs in two deliberate phases: first a research-backed Dossier Jimmy reacts to (his round-1 answers are saved in it, then frozen), then The Full Analysis — the robust, live document those answers produced, which he keeps sharpening with more notes.
