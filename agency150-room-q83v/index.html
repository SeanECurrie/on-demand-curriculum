What this is, and how it works

It started with a couple of casual sentences from Jimmy about a company called Agency150 and a meeting where he might invest. The goal isn’t to tell Jimmy what to do — it’s to empower his own decision with verified facts, clearly separating what the company claims from what’s been independently confirmed. The work is built in two deliberate phases: a research-backed dossier Jimmy reacts to, then a final analysis sharpened by his own input.
