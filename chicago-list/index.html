How I counted, and what the blanks mean

Your spec named four acceptable sources for a head count: the provider list inside the booking page, the team page on the website, Instagram or Facebook, and Google Business photos or posts. I used the first three. I did not use Yelp descriptions, follower counts, photos, or review mentions. When a team page listed staff across several locations without splitting them, or when the roster only shows inside a booking widget, the count is blank and the detail is in Notes.

Why the verified share is small

Most independent salons do not publish a roster anywhere. The booking widgets many of them use, Boulevard, Meevo, Zenoti, Phorest, Vagaro, Booker, Booksy and Square, only show the staff list after you pick a service, which is why "unknown" and "unverified" appear together so often. Of the tools I ran into, only Fresha showed a roster without opening the widget. That is a fact about the market, not about the effort, and it is the reason the unverified pile is still worth working.
