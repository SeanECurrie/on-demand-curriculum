◆ Group supervision, as a de-risk engine
Revenue that isn't a clinical hour. Supervise pre-licensed associates in a group — 6–8 at ~$40–75 each = $240–600 for one two-hour block, not you seeing a client 1-on-1. FEE SURVEYS '25
A pipeline of clinicians who stay. Pre-licensed associates need your supervised hours to get licensed — stickier than a lateral hire, cheaper while they ramp, and they grow into the staff that fills your caseload. A renewable inflow, not one-off hires.
Your favorite work becomes the moat. The mentoring you'd do anyway is the engine that both diversifies income and makes the practice resilient to exactly the shock you just took.
