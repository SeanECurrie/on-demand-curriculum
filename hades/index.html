The logic — why these win

Four ideas the whole plan rests on

🎯 Focus out-scales spread

A Level-2 Common = a Level-1 Rare; each rarity step is roughly a free +1 level. Stack one god and your boons climb tiers; sample everyone and you cap out weak. This is why "commit by room 3" and "Pom one boon" exist.

☣️ Status + amplifier = passive room-melt

Apply a status, then take a boon that exploits it. Dionysus' Bad Influence = +damage to everything while 3+ foes are Hungover. Aphrodite's Sweet Surrender = Weak foes take +dmg from all sources. The room dies while you just reposition.

Ideal: tag a whole pack with one status, then your normal hits all do bonus damage at once.

🛡️ Deflect = defense and offense in one button

Athena's deflect doesn't just block ranged attacks — it reflects them as your damage and marks foes Exposed. In a stage full of lava-throwers (Asphodel), their attack becomes your opener.

📍 Zoning decouples damage from danger

The dream Shield setup lets you deal damage while you're nowhere near the fight. Park a damage source, then block and kite. You stay safe; the build does the killing. That's the playstyle you already love — formalized.
