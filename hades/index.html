The 3 rules that never change
1
Always grab Athena's Divine Dash. A dash that deflects attacks is the best survival in the game — it fits every build.
2
Pom your ONE core boon. Never spread Poms of Power around — dump them into your single main damage boon.
3
Commit by room 3. Pick your god early and stack it. Focus beats variety, every run.
Between runs — where your stuff goes

Spend it in this order

You've already nailed the two best Mirror buys (Death Defiance + the extra dash). Here's the priority for everything else.

🟣
Darkness spend first

→ Mirror of Night. You have Death Defiance r1 + Greater Reflex. Now save toward Death Defiance rank 2 (500) — a 2nd extra life is what gets you the escape.

🦴
Chthonic Keys

→ unlock more Mirror talents. At 5 keys the next tier opens. (You have 3 — save 2 more.)

🩸
Titan Blood

→ weapon Aspects. Next one you earn, put it toward Aspect of Zeus on the Shield (costs 2). That's your ranged dream — see below.

🍶
Nectar

→ gift NPCs for Keepsakes. Give one to Zeus for the Thunder Signet (fishes your lightning build). Spread the rest to meet everyone.

💎

→ House Contractor upgrades. Do the cheap useful ones; not urgent.

🪙
Obols in-run only

Spend them inside a run at Charon's shop — they vanish when you die. Never "save" Obols.

In a run — the boon flow

Build the murder machine

Start weapon select
  • ▸Keepsake: Thunder Signet (Zeus) to fish lightning — or Old Spiked Collar (+HP) while learning.
Rooms 1–3 pick ONE engine
  • ★Zeus on Special (Thunder Flourish) → lightning build
  • ●Dionysus on Special (Drunken Flourish) → Hangover / poison build
  • ★Athena's Divine Dash — grab it ANY time it shows (deflect = survival)
Build out
  • ▸Stack your core god on Attack/Special
  • ★Daedalus Hammer → take Explosive Return (the Shield's best)
  • ▸Grab HP (Centaur Hearts) + any Athena defense
  • ▸Pom of Power → all into your ONE core boon

  • ★Zeus + Dionysus = Scintillating Feast — lightning inside the poison fog. Nasty AoE.
Avoid
  • ✕Poseidon on the Special — knockback scatters foes out of your disc/lightning
  • ✕Taking one boon from every god (weak scattershot)
Your build target
The ranged-zoning dream

Aspect of Zeus + Explosive Return

AspectZeus — turns your Special into a Blitz Disc you park on a target; it deals rapid damage while you move & attack freely.
HammerExplosive Return — catching the disc deals AoE + re-fires your Special's boon.
Special boonZeus (lightning) or Dionysus (Hangover) — turns the parked disc into a constant AoE-status engine.
Why this one: it's exactly the playstyle you already fell in love with — block, position, and attack from afar. The Blitz Disc is a permanent version of the block-then-shoot feeling, built to scale.
